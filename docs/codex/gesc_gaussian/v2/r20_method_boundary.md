# R20 method boundary and next incomplete question

This is an interpretation of completed R20 evidence and a read-only ownership
audit, not adoption of a new algorithm or release of an experiment.

The stationary raw-GESC field is not established as a local attracting root at
the observed D candidate centers. The trajectory can still remain confined due
to closed-loop nonholonomic/dither behavior. A new method should explicitly
consider evidence of persistent trajectory trapping for Gaussian escape rather
than assert that recurrence proves a mathematical minimum. Raw cost ranking and
continuous augmented SEARCH direction confidence remain separate requirements.

The smallest candidate change belongs in existing recurrent_geometry,
moving_evidence, supervisor snapshot, fill-runtime and lifecycle-validator owners.
The recurring detector already checks sustained confinement/circling/oscillation,
drift, residuals, source gaps and SEARCH epochs. Existing moving verification
also requires comparable rotations, negative raw-cost bounds and angular
amplitude greater than three times profile disagreement. That amplitude rule
appears at multiple consumers; a new policy must carry an explicit typed identity
through those owners. Do not fake `informative`, alter old snapshots, or bypass
unselected historical contracts. Existing clipped covariance/curvature and depth
floors are fill design operations, not evidence that trapping or a minimum exists.

A prospective trapping policy would need authenticated pre-intervention recurrent
support from actual SEARCH, fresh pose/control/objective authority, bounded
confinement/persistence and exclusion of commanded verification/escape orbits.
Preserve synchronized raw observations, comparable rotations, finite negative
cost intervals and uncertainty, candidate ranking, fill association and count
limits. Test drifting/slow-traversal and transient-pause negatives, interrupted
authority, commanded orbits and changing objectives. A flat/slope/saddle field
label alone does not determine whether a particular closed-loop trajectory is
trapped. Preserve the bounded fill/escape/ACK/registry and final-zero lifecycle.

All eight retained verification windows begin after confirmation and the centered
VERIFY intervention; they contain zero pre-confirmation samples. Four are C/PDE
and four are D/recurrent. All have compatible geometry and negative-cost summaries
but fail angular-information admission. They can support policy/snapshot/fill
compatibility and cost-quality checks. They cannot independently establish
pre-intervention trapping or successful new-policy escape. Use the four D windows
with their original pre-VERIFY recurrent receipts as the first selected check;
keep C as an unselected comparison, not four more positive examples.

Any resulting method revision requires a prospective plan, bounded component
controls and credible visible integrated behavior before another matrix. R20's
failures, source pins, datasets and cutoffs remain unchanged. No new policy,
experiment, runtime selector, physical work or commit is authorized by this note
beyond the user's existing general simulation-development authority.
